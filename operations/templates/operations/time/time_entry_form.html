Quick Help
Time Tracking Fields

Work Hours: Paid time spent on organization activities
Volunteer Hours: Unpaid time donated to the organization
Travel Hours: Time spent traveling to/from work locations

Travel Information

Travel Miles: Round trip miles driven for organization work
Format: Enter as decimal (e.g., 25.5 miles)

Cost Tracking

Travel Meal Costs: Food expenses while traveling
Billeting Costs: Lodging/accommodation expenses
Purchases: Equipment, supplies, or other items bought

Purchase Explanation

Required when purchases > $0
Explain what was purchased and why it was necessary for the mission.

Example: "Purchased 2 boxes of batteries ($15.99) for handheld radios that ran out of power during extended operations."

Data Entry Tips
  • Use 0.25 hour increments (15 min intervals)
  • At least one hour field must be > 0
  • All cost fields accept dollars and cents
  • Date auto-fills with today's date
  • Incident selection is optional
Organization Context

Time entries are associated with your currently selected organization. Switch organizations in the main dashboard if needed.

Pro Tip: Save time by filling out one complete day, then you can copy and edit for similar days!
